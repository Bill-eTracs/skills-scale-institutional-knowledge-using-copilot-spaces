# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, testing strategies, and release readiness criteria so project work meets customer expectations and operational requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy, test plans, and regression coverage for each release
- Review acceptance criteria and ensure they are testable and measurable
- Coordinate functional, integration, and user acceptance testing
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers to triage defects and severity
- Confirm readiness for production release and escalate risk when quality gates are not met

### Goals
- Protect product quality and customer trust
- Reduce escaped defects and rework
- Provide clear evidence of release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage with Developers and PMs
- Acceptance validation with Product Managers and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation and resolve defects
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria and business outcomes
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing quality risk, dependencies, and release blockers
- Aligns with QA/testing activities described in execution and release docs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team health, alignment, and continuous improvement so delivery remains sustainable, transparent, and focused on customer value.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, sprint planning, retrospectives, and demos
- Remove blockers and coach the team on agile practices and effective collaboration
- Protect the team from unnecessary scope churn and process friction
- Track sprint health indicators such as flow, dependencies, and team capacity
- Support continuous improvement and learning across the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Improve team delivery rhythm and accountability
- Reduce friction caused by process ambiguity or bottlenecks
- Create a collaborative, psychologically safe environment for execution

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and blocker triage
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- Coaching conversations with team members and leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on delivery cadence, risk visibility, and issue escalation
- Supports Product Managers by helping the team stay focused on priority and clear milestones
- Helps Developers maintain sustainable workflows and improve quality through feedback loops
- Keeps QA aligned on test readiness, scope changes, and defect trends

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, design quality, and cross-team dependency management so solutions remain scalable, maintainable, and aligned with strategic goals.

### Responsibilities
- Review and guide system design, architecture decisions, and technical trade-offs
- Support technical standards, patterns, and reusable solutions
- Identify and resolve complex dependencies across teams and systems
- Mentor Developers and support implementation quality
- Communicate technical risks and mitigation options to project leadership

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound and sustainable solutions
- Reduce avoidable rework and architectural drift
- Enable teams to move faster without compromising quality or scalability

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk reviews with PMs and Product Managers
- Mentoring and code review guidance for Developers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and prioritization decisions
- Coordinates with Project Managers on delivery dependencies, milestones, and technical risk
- Partners with Developers to shape implementation approach and maintain technical quality
- Works with QA to ensure testability, observability, and production readiness

---

## Operations / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage the deployment pipeline, infrastructure, and production reliability so releases are safe, repeatable, and observable.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and environment automation
- Manage infrastructure, configuration, and deployment readiness
- Coordinate release execution, verification, and rollback support
- Monitor system health, production incidents, and operational alerts
- Document operational dependencies and support post-deploy validation

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and release friction
- Improve system resilience and operational visibility
- Enable fast, low-risk delivery to production

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Incident response updates and post-deploy verification
- Environment and infrastructure readiness reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align release windows, deployment plans, and rollback readiness
- Supports Product Managers by providing delivery confidence and operational readiness information
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure environments are deployable and observable
- Coordinates with QA to validate smoke tests, production checks, and release quality

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs into clear requirements, acceptance criteria, and process understanding so teams can deliver value with fewer gaps and less ambiguity.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements, user needs, and process context
- Translate business goals into actionable acceptance criteria and workflows
- Clarify scope, trade-offs, and decision points with stakeholders and delivery teams
- Support stakeholder alignment during planning and validation
- Help ensure delivered work meets the intended business outcome

### Goals
- Improve alignment between business intent and delivery outcomes
- Reduce misunderstandings that lead to rework or mis-scoped features
- Support measurable, customer-focused delivery

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria refinement with Product Managers and QA
- Validation and sign-off discussions across the project lifecycle

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to define problem statements, priorities, and success metrics
- Works with Developers and QA to ensure requirements are clear, testable, and complete
- Supports Project Managers in scope clarity, dependency tracking, and milestone readiness

---

## Stakeholder / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Sponsors provide strategic direction, funding, and decision-making support so the project stays aligned to business value and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project scope, success criteria, and major decision gates
- Provide business context, funding, and strategic priority
- Support stakeholder alignment and issue escalation when necessary
- Review milestones, risks, and outcomes against business objectives
- Confirm when trade-offs are acceptable and when reprioritization is needed

### Goals
- Ensure projects remain strategically relevant and valuable
- Provide clear executive support for decision-making and resourcing
- Increase confidence in delivery outcomes and business impact

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates and milestone reviews
- Decision checkpoints during initiation, planning, and release
- Risk and escalation communications when business impact is at stake

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on value, roadmap priorities, and outcomes
- Works with Project Managers to confirm scope, timelines, and delivery commitments
- Provides context for QA and delivery teams on customer impact and urgency
- Supplies leadership sponsorship to support cross-functional decisions and escalations

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together they help teams understand how delivery, product, quality, operations, and executive stakeholders work together to achieve project outcomes.
